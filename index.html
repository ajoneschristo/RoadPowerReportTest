⚡ Road Power Report Settings
Shared with ePass Viewer — only this app's settings are written back
In the app
Tick the reports to show and use the arrows to set their order. The reports in the downloaded files are chosen on the Exports tab. Share sends a picture of the tab that's open, so it follows the app.
Pinned months are the months you pin with ☆ Show in Saved days; This month is the month on screen; FY is the financial year so far — 1 April up to the report's latest day — in the Power Sector, Sourcewise and MCL reports. Each report answers separately depending on whether its day-by-day switch is on, so a wide day-by-day view can drop the summary columns while the compact view keeps them.
Party Code Party Name ⛓ Power R1 Nil R2
Power — treated as power sector, its figures are written to the history file. R1 — appears as a row in Road Despatch to Power Sector. Nil — keeps that row even when the customer lifted nothing, with its figures shown as ·; without it, a customer with no despatch in any period on show is left out. The box under Nil ticks it for every R1 customer. R2 — gets a block in the Customer report. Ticking R1 or R2 ticks Power automatically. One row per party code, so a party with several codes needs each code ticked.
Broad groups of Power customers. The Power Sector report lists each group's customers together, in the order below, with a subtotal for the group unless that is switched off; with colours on, each group's rows take its colour (tap the dot to change it). On the Google Sheet tab each group gets its own start column. This is separate from merging codes into one customer (⛓).
Customers
▲ ▼ set the order of the customers in the report. Codes: comma-separated colliery codes (e.g. 5046 or 5046,5075,5077). Leave In total ticked for an ordinary colliery column. Untick it for a roll-up column such as Talcher, which already contains other columns and would otherwise be counted twice — the report heads it “Subtotal (Talcher)”. At least one column must stay ticked, or the Total has nothing to add.

For every PDF type. Only matters when the group totals table is on (Customers tab → Customer Groups); otherwise each table has a page of its own.
Sends each Power customer's daily road despatch into fixed cells of a Google Sheet, through a small Apps Script you deploy once. When it's on, every Save also sends the saved dates of the latest month, and a Sheet button next to PDF, Excel and HTML sends by hand. Figures go as stored, to two decimals — format the cells in the sheet however you like.
Group start columns
Rows
CustomerGroupRow
Give each customer group its Start col — the column of the 1st of the month — and each customer its Row; a customer's group is set on the Customers tab. Day n then goes (n − 1) × common step columns on: start col E with a step of 4 puts day 2 in I and day 3 in M. A customer given its own start col before groups existed keeps it until its group has one. Leave Row empty to keep a customer out of the sheet. Customers or codes given the same cells are added together before sending. The month is always the latest one with data, in the history or the loaded file — when that's 1 October, the figures go into the October days. A merged customer sends its combined figure to one row; tick Separate rows to give each of its codes a row of its own. The list follows the Power ticks on the Customers tab.
