In the app
In exports — PDF, Excel & HTML
Tick the reports to show and use the arrows to set their order — separately for the tabs in the app and for the exported files. Share sends a picture of the tab that's open, so it follows the app.
The first pair covers the months you pin with ☆ Show in Saved days; the second pair covers the month on screen. Each report answers separately depending on whether its day-by-day switch is on, so a wide day-by-day view can drop the summary columns while the compact view keeps them.
Party Code Party Name ⛓ Power R1 Nil R2
Power — treated as power sector, its figures are written to the history file. R1 — appears as a row in Road Despatch to Power Sector. Nil — keeps that row even when the customer lifted nothing, with its figures shown as ·; without it, a customer with no despatch in any period on show is left out. The box under Nil ticks it for every R1 customer. R2 — gets a block in the Customer report. Ticking R1 or R2 ticks Power automatically. One row per party code, so a party with several codes needs each code ticked.
Codes: comma-separated colliery codes (e.g. 5046 or 5046,5075,5077). Leave In total ticked for an ordinary colliery column. Untick it for a roll-up column such as “Talcher Total”, which already contains other columns and would otherwise be counted twice. At least one column must stay ticked, or the Total has nothing to add.
Rendered detail
High fetches the app's fonts the first time it's used; without a connection it falls back to the PDF's standard fonts.
Sizes are for the reports you'd export right now.
Sends each Power customer's daily road despatch into fixed cells of a Google Sheet, through a small Apps Script you deploy once. When it's on, a Sheet button sits next to PDF, Excel and HTML.
Values
Day n goes in column Day 1 column + (n − 1) × Step — with E and 4, day 2 lands in I, day 3 in M. These are the defaults; a customer only needs its own column or step when its layout differs. The month is the report's — when the latest date is 1 October, the figures go into the October days.
CustomerRowDay 1Step
Leave Row empty to keep a customer out of the sheet. Customers or codes given the same cells are added together before sending. A merged customer sends its combined figure to one row; tick Separate rows to give each of its codes a row of its own. The list follows the Power ticks on the Customers tab.
  1. Open script.google.com → New project, clear the editor and paste the script.
  2. Deploy → New deployment, type Web app. Execute as Me; who has access Anyone.
  3. Deploy, allow the permissions it asks for, and copy the Web app URL (it ends in /exec) into Apps Script URL above.
  4. Tap Test connection. One script serves every month's spreadsheet — just change the URL or tab here.
